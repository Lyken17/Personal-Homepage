I am a Researcher at NVIDIA, fortunately working with Prof. Song Han. Before NVIDIA, I was at MIT (freezing Boston) with Prof. Song Han, Simon Fraser University (warmful Vancouver) with Prof. Brian Funt and Prof. Ping Tan, and Zhejiang University (beautiful Hangzhou).

I am currently leading Humanize (flow harness framework) and KDA (Kernel Design Agents) at NVIDIA. My research focuses on scalable and efficient agents. My mission is five "ones": 100T model + 1K TPS + 1M agents + 1M dockers + 1M mins.

If you find any research interests that we might share, feel free to drop me an email. I am always open to potential collaborations.
